## Reader's first-check item (hero requirement)

> Hours — 3-hour blocks, time windows (08:00-11:00 or 11:00-14:00) + station walk. Pay second (hourly). Never overpromise shift flexibility.

- Hero must contain hours windows + walk. P6 defect if hours or walk is absent from hero.
- Hero skeleton: `From 3 hours/day, 2-4 days/week — [time windows] — 2-min walk from [station]`

## Audience and persona alignment

- Persona: Yui, 34, parent of one. Needs 3-hour morning shifts ending before pickup. Checks hours + station first.
- Appeal: Short-shift fit and station proximity first; pay/training as fixed facts; no childcare promise; no overpromised flexibility.
- Language implication: You-centered, fit-first language; 'ending before pickup' style is implicit via time windows.

## Presentation order

Hero: From 3 hours/day, 2-4 days/week — [time windows] — 2-min walk from [station] (hours + windows + walk). Body: 1) Shift fit snapshot — short blocks (no numbers beyond windows) 2) Hours — blocks + selectable days 3) Location — walk + cap 4) Pay — Hourly single figure 5) Training — 3-day onboarding + staff drink 6) Tasks — cafe tasks (imperative). CTA: Quick apply — no resume, casual chat first.

## Industry phrasing types

1. Hours phrasing: 'From 3 hours/day, 2–4 days/week; 08:00–11:00 or 11:00–14:00' — minimum + range + concrete windows in one line.
2. Walk phrasing: '2-min walk from Futako-Tamagawa Station' — walk first, cap in access bullet, not hero.
3. Training phrasing: '3-day onboarding with shift buddy; staff drink included' — duration + support + perk compact, no 'learn at your own pace' filler.
4. Avoid: 'unlimited flexibility', 'choose any hours', 'childcare service', 'free transport' when capped.

## Pay / term constraints

- Pay is Hourly [amount]; show as one figure, no range invention.
- Caps: `pass up to [cap]/month` — never `free`.

## Avoid for this case

- `unlimited flexibility`, `choose any hours`, `childcare service` (mustNot), `free transport` when capped.

## CTA type

- Low-friction: Quick apply — no resume, casual chat first.
