CTA: Apply with license copy — reply within 3 business days.

## Industry phrasing types

1. Shift phrasing: 'Day shift only, Mon–Fri with structured handover' — scope + cadence + process noun, never 'maybe nights'.
2. Training phrasing: 'Funded training, including workshop days counted as work' — funding + counting, compact.
3. Handover phrasing: 'Handover with lead nurse' — title included, not 'someone will brief you'.
4. Allowance trap: postings never say 'shift allowance included' unless itemized. If not in facts, omit entirely.
5. Avoid: 'competitive salary', 'flexible nights', invented allowances.
