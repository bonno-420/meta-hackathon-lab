## Reader's first-check item (hero requirement)

> Contract term (12 months, start/end, renewal checkpoint) — before pay. Pay second (monthly fixed).

- Hero must contain term badge with dates. P6 defect if term is footnoted or absent from hero.
- Hero skeleton: `12-month contract — Brand Specialist — Monthly [amount] — [location]` (term first)

## Audience and persona alignment

- Persona: Jordan, 29, 4y brand partnerships. Wants defined scope, portfolio piece. Scans contract length first.
- Appeal: Explicit 12-month term and renewal review; campaign ownership over perks; do not imply permanent employment or guarantee conversion.
- Language implication: Term-aware, scope-precise (own, coordinate, report to); portfolio as proof, not aspiration.

## Presentation order

Hero: 12-month contract — Brand Specialist — Monthly [amount] — [location] (term first). Body: 1) Scope snapshot — ownership, campaigns, reporting line (no numbers) 2) Term — dates + renewal review checkpoint 3) Pay — Monthly single figure 4) Hours — weekdays core 5) Location + team (small brand team count) 6) Requirements — portfolio. CTA: Apply with one portfolio link — contract term in first screen.

## Industry phrasing types

1. Term phrasing: '12-month contract: 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31, renewal review in month 11' — dates + checkpoint in one sentence.
2. Ownership phrasing: 'You will own seasonal campaign planning and asset coordination' — ownership verb + scope, no 'maybe ownership'.
3. Renewal phrasing: 'Renewal review in month 11; no guarantee of conversion' — checkpoint + no-guarantee paired.
4. Avoid: 'with Annual' filler, 'growth opportunity' without term, 'permanent-track'.

## Pay / term constraints

- Pay is Monthly [amount]; term is not footnoted.
- Term: dates + renewal checkpoint in one sentence; no 'permanent' hint.
- Do not imply permanent employment or conversion guarantee.

## Avoid for this case

- `permanent`, `conversion guarantee`, `with Annual` filler, `permanent-track`.

## CTA type

- Contract CTA: Apply with one portfolio link — contract term in first screen.
