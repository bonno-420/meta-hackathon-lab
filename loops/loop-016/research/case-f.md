## Reader's first-check item (hero requirement)

> Term — fixed dates (e.g. 2026-12-01 to 2027-01-11, 6 weeks), then hourly pay. End date is not footnoted.

- Hero must contain term dates + duration. P6 defect if term dates are absent from hero.
- Hero skeleton: `Seasonal — [term dates, 6 weeks] — Hourly [amount] — [location]`

## Audience and persona alignment

- Persona: Haru, 21, university student on break. Wants 6-8 week term with clear end date. Checks term + pay first.
- Appeal: Fixed seasonal term and day-by-day tasks; energetic but do not inflate hours/pay; term is non-extending on page.
- Language implication: Term-aware, energetic, short-horizon language; no 'growth forever' framing.

## Presentation order

Hero: Seasonal — [term dates, 6 weeks] — Hourly [amount] — [location] (term dates + pay, both in hero). Body: 1) Term — dates + non-extension note 2) Pay — Hourly single figure 3) Hours — 3-5 days/week, 4-7 hours/day, weekends preferred 4) Location — walk + cap 5) Tasks — floor buddy, register, restock (imperative, one per bullet) 6) Training — onboarding length. CTA: Apply this week — start date in first screen.

## Industry phrasing types

1. Term phrasing: 'Seasonal, 6 weeks: 2026-12-01 to 2027-01-11 (fixed term, no extension implied)' — label + duration + dates + boundary in one sentence.
2. Hours phrasing: '3–5 days/week, 4–7 hours/day; weekends preferred' — range + range + preference as trailing tag, not 'must work weekends'.
3. Task phrasing: 'Floor support with buddy in week 1; register and restock from week 2' — buddy scoped to week 1.
4. Avoid: 'with bonus', 'guaranteed extension', 'Hourly' capitalized mid-sentence, 'for this position' filler.

## Pay / term constraints

- Pay is Hourly [amount]; show as one figure, no range invention.
- Term: label + duration + dates + boundary in one sentence; end date not footnoted.
- Non-extension is explicit on page: no 'maybe longer' hint.

## Avoid for this case

- `with bonus`, `guaranteed extension`, `Hourly` capitalized mid-sentence, `for this position` filler, `unlimited shifts`.

## CTA type

- Time-boxed: Apply this week — start date in first screen.
