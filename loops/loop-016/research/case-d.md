## Industry phrasing types

1. Dispatched framing: 'Employed by [agency], working at [client site]' — two clauses, never collapsed to one location.
2. Days phrasing: '3–5 days/week selectable; your coordinator confirms the schedule each [cadence]' — range + selector + confirmation cadence.
3. Access phrasing: 'Shuttle from Kawaguchi Station; pass up to 10,000 JPY/month' — shuttle first, cap second.
4. Avoid: 'direct-hire potential', 'factory-direct', 'guaranteed conversion', merging employer/worksite into one line.

## Pay / term constraints

- Caps: `pass up to [cap]/month` — never `free`.
